# Risk Register

> **Status:** Current · refreshed 2026-11-08 (R-12 to R-20 re-read against the work since 2026-10-30 and updated where it changed; R-21 to R-24 added from the vendor queue, sessions, the dependency audit and the Android app; R-01 to R-11 date from September 2026 and were re-read, not re-rated). Ratings are proposed defaults, not measured: qualitative High, Medium and Low. The lead's confirmation of them is the open point of `WI-MGMT-002`.

Every technical risk here traces to a specific ADR, an evidence record, or an entry in `docs/governance/open-items-register.md`. This document reformats them into a prioritized likelihood and impact view for planning; it does not replace the register as the source of remediation detail.

Status values: **Open** (needs action or watching), **Mitigated** (a response is in place; the risk can recur), **Accepted** (accepted on purpose), **Closed** (no longer a risk).

## Notes

- **R-10 is the one risk not already captured elsewhere** when this register was written: a schedule and scope risk, rated High deliberately.
- **R-12 to R-24 come from evidence**, not from the September plan: each cites the record that shows it. R-16 and R-13 are about trust in the project's own checks, which matters because the repository is meant to show how work is verified.
- Risks rated Low/Low are listed for completeness and traceability, not because they need active attention.
- Not yet on the list because there is no evidence: vendor or customer data-protection incidents (no real data exists), Stripe account and compliance risk (no integration), and cost (nothing is billed; `budget-cost-estimate.md` is an estimate and says so).
