# Supersession Note — Minimum Sufficient Context vs. Targeted Sufficient Context

**Date:** 2026-09-28
**Type:** New note. No existing item edited.
**Status:** Proposed. Confirm on merge.

## Change

Exploration items ART-015, ART-016, CONT-005 and ART-020 (and the continuity
audit's "bounded exploration" refinement) use "minimum sufficient context."
V2-010, ART-023 and `renderer-rules-v0.1.md` §5–6, §19 explicitly reverse this:
the target is **targeted sufficient context, not minimum context**.

## Disposition

- Current principle: targeted sufficient context.
- Superseded wording: "minimum sufficient context" in the sections above.
- Not deleted. Add a `Superseded by V2-010` marker to those sections; do not rewrite them.
- Record the decision in the worklog's decision log.
