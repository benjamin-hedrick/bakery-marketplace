## What the session did

Nothing is pushed; everything is committed on the local branch. The five Dependabot alerts (register 162) were fixed: a lockfile update plus a scoped `minimatch` override in the Android app, and `minimatch` 11 pinned under `eslint` and `typescript-eslint` in the BFF and both web apps; every audit reports 0, and all 15 Android flows passed in one cold run (9 min 15 s) on the resulting tree. Register items 44, 90, 111, 130, 132, 158, 160, 170, 172, 173, 176 and 177 were closed on the lead's decisions; for 111 the check found three import-order errors in the tools tests and they were fixed. The register walk-through listed the rest of the items that wait for the lead.

## Errors the assistant made, and what caught them

- A commit message said register 173 was closed when the lead had closed only 172, 176 and 177: caught on rereading the message, and the commit was amended before anything was pushed.
- A draft line in register 90 claimed the ruleset on `main` made the docs check required, which had not been checked: removed before the commit.
- An empty shell heredoc was run against the rule in `CLAUDE.md`: no effect, noticed afterwards.

## How to read the numbers

- **Relative cost is a size, not a price.** 0.48 is under half the base session.
- **The screen's line count is not the repository's.** It is far below what the commits changed (the lockfiles alone changed by hundreds of lines), so it appears to count only some of the edits.
- **The session was mostly waiting** for the long runs (the Android flows) and for the lead's decisions; the screen counts the assistant's time only.
- **No claim is made about value per unit of cost.** The human review time is not in these figures.
