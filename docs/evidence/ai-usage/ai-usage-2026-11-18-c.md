## What the session did

The merged `feature/hardening-followups` was cleaned up locally and a new branch started. Register 199 was closed: GitHub withdrew the picomatch advisory, so the dependency audit's list of accepted advisories was emptied (the lead approved; the audit passes without it). Register 184 was fixed with the option the lead chose: the resilient cache marks a key whose delete did not reach Redis and serves it from its fallback until a delete lands, so a failed invalidation no longer brings the old status back when the breaker closes; the `stale-cache` chaos run on the local stack went from 117 of 176 stale samples to none. The vendor's listing and stock screens were drafted, the lead decided the seven open points, and they became two Ready items, `WI-CAT-007` (catalog and BFF: an edit precondition on a separate edit version, stock settings without the quantity, an idempotent create) and `WI-CAT-008` (the screens); drafting them found register 208 (a listing edit could undo another edit or undo sales). Gates on the final tree: node gates, the Linux check and the secret scan passed.

## Errors the assistant made, and what caught them

Register 201 holds it, (10) of the 2026-11-18 sessions: the dated note on ADR-0002 was written through a heredoc, against the repository's rule; the delimiter was quoted, so nothing ran, and the note's text was checked afterwards. Also caught before a commit: the evidence row first said the fix was committed unchanged after the chaos run, when a doc comment had been added after it; corrected before the commit.

## How to read the numbers

- **Relative cost is a size, not a price.** 3.14 is about three times the base session: one live chaos run, a small change in a shared library, two specs and the gates.
- **No compaction and no cache rebuild**: every request after the first read its context from the cache.
- **Most of the wall time was the chaos run, the Linux check and the time the lead took to decide**; the screen counts the assistant's time only.
- **No claim is made about value per unit of cost.** The human review time is not in these figures.
