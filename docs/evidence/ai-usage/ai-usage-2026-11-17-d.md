## What the session did

Register 200 was resolved: the two Android flows that shared the Almond Croissant's four units now each buy a seeded listing of their own, made to order (Walnut Banana Bread, Walnut Danish). All fifteen Android flows passed in one cold run, and the catalog tests passed. The current plan's "In flight" section was corrected. The two commits are local and were made **without** the Node gates, the secret scan or the real-stack smoke, at the lead's instruction to stop; register 201 lists them as to be run before the push.

## Errors the assistant made, and what caught them

- After the flow run had passed, the assistant waited on the background shell for about 75 minutes instead of reading the run's log. A Gradle daemon started by the app build held the shell's output open. Caught by the lead asking why the shell was still running; `gradlew --stop` released it.
- A recursive search over the apps included `node_modules` and ran into the two-minute timeout. Noticed by the assistant; the search was redone with the Grep tool.
- The lead stopped the session because of the time lost; the mistakes are recorded in register 201, which later sessions add to.

## How to read the numbers

- **Relative cost is a size, not a price.** 1.85 is a little under twice the base session.
- **Most of the wall time was the Android flow run and the hung shell**; the screen counts the assistant's time only.
- **No claim is made about value per unit of cost.** The human review time is not in these figures.
