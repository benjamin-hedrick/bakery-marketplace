## What the BFF's journal shows

Flow 07 (three injected faults, nothing else):

- `layer=signal fault=switch:mobile-flows-07 key=listing-cardamom-latte-001`: opening the Cardamom Honey Latte armed the second scenario. The signal, the aimed `where` match on the customer (read from the token) and `failFirstAttempts` all worked on the real device path.
- `layer=bffCatalog fault=failFirst key=getPreferences:getPreferences` and `layer=bffOrdering fault=failFirst key=listPaymentMethods:listPaymentMethods`: Nora's first allergy-profile read and first payment-method read failed once each, and the retry of the payment methods went through, as the scenario says. The flow's assertions held: "We could not check this order against your allergies", never "no conflicts", the place button off until the payment methods were asked for again, then on, and the order placed. The BFF was not stopped or restarted.
- Not in the journal: a `checkCart` failure. The scenario names one, but with the allergy profile already failed the app made no check call to fail, so that rule was not exercised here.

Flow 11 (one injected fault):

- `layer=bffOrdering fault=failFirst key=placeOrder:mobile-<idempotency key>`: the first `placeOrder` failed once; the app kept the order and its idempotency key, the retry with the same key went through, and the flow's assertions on the order screen held. The journal shows one failure and no second one, so exactly one `placeOrder` was refused and one went through.

## What this does and does not show

- Shown: the aimed faults (`where`: operation, customer from the token, payload pattern), `failFirstAttempts` and a signal that switches scenario, all driven by the BFF on a real emulator against the real stack; flow 07 no longer needs the BFF stopped (register 173); flow 11 keeps its retry check on a scenario (register 172).
- Not shown by these two runs: the `reset` signal and the `enable`/`disable` signal actions (built, unit-tested, used by no flow); a second consecutive run of a flow against the same stack (each run starts the stack afresh); the `checkCart` rule above.
- Not changed: the flows' control server, the web app's `x-fixture` and `x-delay-ms` headers (register: for review, not to be extended).
