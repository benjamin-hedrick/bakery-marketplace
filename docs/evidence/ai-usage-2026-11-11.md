## How to read the numbers

- **Relative cost is a size, not a price.** 3.39 means about three and a third times the base session, and says nothing about money.
- **The screen's line count is not the repository's.** It also counts edit scripts and scratch files.
- **No claim is made about value per unit of cost.** The human review time is not in these figures.
- **A caveat from the screen itself.** It attributed 82% of the last day's use to continuing in one context across different tasks, and 23% to the size of `CLAUDE.md` (about 5k tokens on every request, before it was cut in this session). The usage figures after the cut are not yet known.
